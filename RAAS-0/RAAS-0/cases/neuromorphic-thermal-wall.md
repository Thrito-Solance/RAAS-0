## Mapping

A self-assembling titanate or perovskite neuromorphic composite is a multi-graph substrate X_N = ⟨M_N, ρ_E⟩: conductive vacancy filaments are edges tracking ionic flux J_i, with assembly index given by the non-trivial cycle-depth of the memory pathways.

## The cliff

Changing a filament's resistance state carries a mandatory entropy overhead governed by Ω (Onsager phase overlap). If the composite is cycled faster than its natural ionic and thermal diffusion timelines (T_μ > Δt_diffusion), localized Joule heating cannot clear; the required join breaches the matrix sheaf limit, the task maps to 0_Res, and the ceramic locks into a permanently short-circuited conducting block.

## Verdict

The material's maximum operating frequency is not an engineering parameter but a thermodynamic identity: it is the rate at which μ can be paid.

Falsifier: a cycling protocol that outpaces Δt_diffusion indefinitely without lockup.
