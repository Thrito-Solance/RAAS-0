# Case: The Compute Density Cliff — Recursively Self-Improving AGI

License: AGPL-3.0 — see LICENSE and NOTICE.md.

## Mapping

A self-improving AGI is a hybrid object executing a discrete operadic chain of self-rewriting tasks: E_γ(X_N) →(μ_N) X_(N+1), each generation priced by F_R.

## The cliff

To compile generation N+1, the meta-optimizer γ_N executes a combinatorial search through graph space; the directed assembly distance AI(X_(N+1) | X_N) scales factorially. The infimum of the required resource lattice jumps discretely past the physical cluster capacity: inf {R} ≻ R_cluster, the step maps to 0_Res, and the intelligence explosion freezes mid-loop.

## The backlog wall

Compiling costs real time steps (T_μ ≻ 0) during which hardware accumulates environmental noise (η ≠ ∅). When T_μ exceeds the hardware coherence threshold, a decoder backlog forms: noise cascades faster than the recovery morphism can clear it, Trace(X_N · P_C) → 0, and the model dissolves into non-functional statistical noise.

## Verdict

Recursive self-improvement is bounded by two cliffs of the same shape: a discrete substrate wall (space) and a syndrome backlog wall (time). Both are Law 5 events.

Falsifier: any demonstrated self-improvement loop whose per-generation cost curve stays sub-linear in assembly distance while holding coherence.
